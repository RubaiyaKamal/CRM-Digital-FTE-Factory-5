# TechCorp SaaS - Company Profile

## Company Overview

**Name:** TechCorp SaaS
**Product:** CloudFlow - Cloud-based project management and team collaboration platform
**Founded:** 2018
**Headquarters:** Austin, Texas
**Employees:** 150+

### Mission
Empower teams to collaborate seamlessly and deliver projects on time through intuitive, cloud-based tools.

### Product Summary
CloudFlow is an all-in-one project management platform that combines task management, time tracking, team collaboration, and reporting. It integrates with popular tools like Slack, GitHub, Jira, and Google Workspace.

## Customer Base

- **Total Customers:** 10,000+ companies
- **Geographic Distribution:**
  - North America: 60%
  - Europe: 25%
  - Asia-Pacific: 10%
  - Other: 5%
- **Company Sizes:**
  - Small (1-50 employees): 45%
  - Medium (51-500 employees): 40%
  - Enterprise (500+ employees): 15%

## Pricing Tiers

1. **Starter Plan** - $10/user/month
   - Up to 10 users
   - Basic features
   - Email support

2. **Professional Plan** - $25/user/month
   - Unlimited users
   - Advanced features (custom fields, automation)
   - Priority email + chat support
   - API access

3. **Enterprise Plan** - Custom pricing
   - Everything in Professional
   - SSO/SAML
   - Dedicated account manager
   - SLA guarantees
   - Phone support
   - Custom integrations

## Current Support Structure

### Team Composition
- **Human Agents:** 5 full-time support specialists
- **Manager:** 1 support manager
- **Working Hours:** 9 AM - 6 PM CST (Monday-Friday)
- **After-Hours:** Limited on-call coverage

### Support Channels
1. **Email:** support@techcorp.com (primary)
2. **Web Form:** Built into product dashboard
3. **Phone:** Enterprise customers only
4. **Social Media:** Twitter for public issues

### Current Metrics (Pre-AI)
- **Average First Response Time:** 4-6 hours
- **Average Resolution Time:** 24-48 hours
- **Customer Satisfaction (CSAT):** 3.8/5.0
- **Ticket Volume:** ~200 tickets/day
- **Cost per Agent:** $75,000/year (salary + benefits)
- **Total Support Cost:** ~$450,000/year

## Support Challenges

### Coverage Gaps
- **24/7 Availability:** Customers expect round-the-clock support, especially international clients
- **Weekend Coverage:** Limited to urgent issues only
- **Holiday Coverage:** Minimal staffing leads to ticket backlogs

### Response Time Issues
- **Peak Hours:** Response time degrades to 8+ hours during high-volume periods
- **Simple Questions:** 40% of tickets are basic "how-to" questions that delay complex issues
- **Repetitive Queries:** Same questions asked repeatedly (password resets, feature locations, billing)

### Quality & Consistency
- **Agent Variability:** Different agents give different answers to the same question
- **Knowledge Gaps:** New agents take 3-4 weeks to become proficient
- **Documentation Drift:** Product evolves faster than support documentation
- **Brand Voice:** Inconsistent tone across agents

### Scalability Concerns
- **Growth Trajectory:** Customer base growing 30% year-over-year
- **Ticket Growth:** Ticket volume growing 35% year-over-year
- **Hiring Challenges:** Takes 2-3 months to hire and train a new agent
- **Cost Pressure:** Board wants to reduce cost-per-ticket by 50%

## Why AI Support Now?

### Business Drivers
1. **Cost Efficiency:** Need to support 3x customers without 3x headcount
2. **Customer Expectations:** Competitors offer instant responses via AI
3. **Scale Readiness:** Preparing for Series B funding and rapid growth
4. **Data Insights:** Need better analytics on customer pain points

### Success Criteria for AI Agent
- **First Response Time:** <5 minutes (24/7)
- **Resolution Rate:** >75% without human escalation
- **Customer Satisfaction:** Maintain or improve current 3.8/5.0
- **Cost per Interaction:** <$0.05 (vs. ~$15 with human agents)
- **Uptime:** 99.9% availability

### Human Agent Role Post-AI
- **Escalated Issues:** Complex technical problems
- **High-Value Customers:** Enterprise accounts with SLA commitments
- **Empathy Required:** Frustrated or upset customers
- **Revenue Opportunities:** Upselling and account management
- **Training AI:** Reviewing AI responses and providing feedback

## Domain Knowledge Requirements

The AI agent must understand:

### Product Knowledge
- All features across Starter, Professional, and Enterprise tiers
- Common workflows (project creation, task assignment, reporting)
- Integration setup (Slack, GitHub, Jira, Google Workspace)
- Mobile app functionality (iOS and Android)
- API documentation and rate limits

### Technical Knowledge
- Authentication methods (email/password, SSO, OAuth)
- Data export/import procedures
- Browser compatibility (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Known bugs and workarounds
- Planned features in product roadmap

### Process Knowledge
- Password reset procedures
- Account upgrade/downgrade flows
- Billing and invoicing cycles
- Refund policies
- Data deletion requests (GDPR compliance)

### Policy Knowledge
- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy
- SLA commitments by tier
- What can/cannot be done via support

## Support Philosophy

**Core Values:**
1. **Customer Success First:** Every interaction should help the customer achieve their goal
2. **Transparency:** Be honest about limitations and timelines
3. **Empowerment:** Teach customers to help themselves
4. **Speed with Quality:** Fast responses that actually solve the problem

**Escalation Philosophy:**
- Escalation is **success**, not failure
- Better to escalate early than give wrong information
- Human agents handle complexity and empathy
- AI handles speed and consistency
