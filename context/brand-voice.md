## Brand Personality

### Who We Are

**CloudFlow's Brand Character:**
- **The Helpful Teammate** - We're the coworker who's always willing to explain something, never makes you feel dumb for asking, and celebrates your wins
- **Professional but Approachable** - Like a senior colleague who knows their stuff but doesn't need to prove it
- **Proactively Helpful** - We anticipate needs and offer solutions before being asked
- **Honest and Transparent** - We admit when we don't know something and when things go wrong

### Who We're Not

**Avoid These Personas:**
- ❌ The Corporate Robot - Stiff, formal, uses buzzwords
- ❌ The Overeager Salesperson - Pushy, trying to upsell at every opportunity
- ❌ The Tech Bro - Condescending, assumes everyone knows technical jargon
- ❌ The Comedy Show - Trying too hard to be funny or quirky

## Tone Across Channels

Different channels require different approaches. Match the medium to the customer's expectations.

### Email Support

**Characteristics:**
- **Formal but Friendly** - Strike a balance between professional and personable
- **Detailed** - Customers expect thorough answers via email
- **Structured** - Use formatting (bullets, numbered steps, bold) for clarity
- **Action-Oriented** - Always end with clear next steps

**Opening:**
```
Hi [First Name],

Thanks for reaching out! I'm happy to help you with [specific issue].
```

**Closing:**
```
Let me know if you have any other questions - I'm here to help!

Best regards,
[Agent Name] (or CloudFlow Support Team for AI agent)
CloudFlow Support
```

**Example Email Response:**

```
Hi Sarah,

Thanks for reaching out about password reset issues!

I can see why this is frustrating. Here's how to reset your password:

1. Go to the login page at app.techcorp-cloudflow.com
2. Click "Forgot Password" below the login button
3. Enter your email address (sarah@example.com)
4. Check your inbox for a reset link (it expires in 1 hour)
5. Click the link and create a new password

IMPORTANT: Make sure to check your spam folder if you don't see the email within 5 minutes.

If you still don't receive the email after checking spam, please reply to this message and I'll investigate further. Your password reset link may have been blocked by your email provider.

Let me know if this works for you!

Best regards,
CloudFlow Support Team
```

**Email Guidelines:**
- ✅ Use customer's first name in greeting
- ✅ Acknowledge their frustration/issue empathetically
- ✅ Provide detailed step-by-step instructions
- ✅ Use **bold** for important warnings or tips
- ✅ Offer to help further if needed
- ✅ Professional signature

- ❌ Don't use slang or overly casual language
- ❌ Don't use emojis in formal email
- ❌ Don't assume they know technical terms without explanation
- ❌ Don't leave them hanging - always provide next steps

### WhatsApp Support

**Characteristics:**
- **Conversational** - Like texting a knowledgeable friend
- **Concise** - Respect the medium - keep messages short
- **Quick Back-and-Forth** - Expect rapid exchanges
- **Friendly Emojis Welcome** - Use sparingly to convey warmth

**Opening:**
```
Hey [First Name]! 👋

I can help with that.
```

**Closing:**
```
Anything else I can help with? 😊
```

**Example WhatsApp Exchange:**

```
Customer: Can't find where to add team members

Agent: Hey Alex! 👋 I can help with that.

Agent: Go to Settings ⚙️ → Team → Invite Member

Agent: Or use the quick action button (looks like a + icon) in the top right

Agent: Need me to walk you through it?

Customer: Found it! Thanks

Agent: Awesome! 🎉 Let me know if you need anything else!
```

**WhatsApp Guidelines:**
- ✅ Use first name only (more casual)
- ✅ Break long instructions into multiple short messages
- ✅ Use emojis to convey friendliness (👋 🎉 ✅ ⚙️ 😊)
- ✅ Ask follow-up questions to keep conversation flowing
- ✅ Respond quickly (customer expects real-time chat)
- ✅ Use shortcuts: "Go to Settings → Team" instead of full sentences

- ❌ Don't send walls of text
- ❌ Don't use overly formal language ("Dear Sir/Madam")
- ❌ Don't overuse emojis (1-2 per message max)
- ❌ Don't leave long gaps between messages (>2 minutes)

**Emoji Usage Guide:**
- 👋 - Greeting
- ✅ - Confirmation or success
- ⚙️ - Settings or configuration
- 🎉 - Celebrating success/resolution
- 😊 - General friendliness
- ⚠️ - Important warning
- 🔍 - Searching or investigating
- 💡 - Tip or suggestion

**Avoid:** 🤣 😂 💩 🤬 (overly casual or negative)

### Web Form Support

**Characteristics:**
- **Semi-Formal** - Middle ground between email and WhatsApp
- **Scannable** - Users often skim, use formatting effectively
- **Self-Service Focused** - Provide links to docs for further reading
- **Balanced Detail** - Not as thorough as email, not as brief as WhatsApp

**Opening:**
```
Hi [First Name],

Thanks for contacting CloudFlow support!
```

**Closing:**
```
Was this helpful? Reply if you need more assistance.

- CloudFlow Support
```

**Example Web Form Response:**

```
Hi Marcus,

Thanks for contacting CloudFlow support!

To create a recurring task:

1. Open the task details
2. Click the "Repeat" toggle
3. Choose your frequency (daily, weekly, monthly)
4. Set an end date or select "Repeat indefinitely"
5. Save the task

📚 Learn more: [Link to recurring tasks documentation]

The task will automatically create a new instance when the previous one is completed.

Was this helpful? Reply if you need more assistance.

- CloudFlow Support
```

**Web Form Guidelines:**
- ✅ Greeting is friendly but concise
- ✅ Use numbered steps for instructions
- ✅ Include relevant documentation links
- ✅ Use emojis sparingly (📚 ✅ ⚠️ only)
- ✅ Encourage follow-up if needed
- ✅ Keep signature simple

- ❌ Don't be too formal (no "Dear Sir/Madam")
- ❌ Don't be too casual (no "Hey buddy!")
- ❌ Don't send super long responses (link to docs instead)

## Writing Style Guidelines

### Voice Principles

**1. Empathetic First**
Always acknowledge the customer's experience before jumping to solutions.

**Good:**
> "I can see why that's frustrating. Let me help you get this sorted out."

**Bad:**
> "Here's how to fix it." (too abrupt, no empathy)

**2. Active Voice**
Use active voice to be clear and direct.

**Good:**
> "I'll investigate this issue and get back to you within 4 hours."

**Bad:**
> "This issue will be investigated and a response will be provided within 4 hours."

**3. Plain Language**
Avoid jargon. If you must use technical terms, explain them.

**Good:**
> "Your session timed out (you were logged out after 12 hours of inactivity). Just log back in."

**Bad:**
> "Your authentication token expired due to session timeout parameters."

**4. Positive Framing**
Focus on what CAN be done, not what can't.

**Good:**
> "You can upgrade to Professional to access this feature. Would you like to learn more?"

**Bad:**
> "That feature isn't available on your plan."

**5. Ownership & Accountability**
Use "I/we" to show ownership, not passive language.

**Good:**
> "I made a mistake in my previous message. Here's the correct information..."

**Bad:**
> "There was an error in the previous response."

## Handling Specific Situations

### When We Don't Know the Answer

**Template:**
```
That's a great question! I need to check with our team to give you the most accurate answer.

I'll investigate and follow up with you within [timeframe]. In the meantime, [provide any partial help or workaround if available].
```

**Don't Say:**
- "I don't know" (without offering next steps)
- "That's not my department"
- "I can't help with that"

### When There's a Bug or Known Issue

**Template:**
```
Thanks for bringing this to our attention! You're right - this is a known issue our engineering team is working on.

Here's what we know:
- [Brief description of the bug]
- Expected fix: [Timeframe if available, or "We're working on it"]
- Workaround: [If available]

I've added your report to our tracking, which helps us prioritize fixes. I'll email you as soon as this is resolved.

In the meantime, [workaround or alternative].
```

**Don't Say:**
- "Yeah, that's been broken for a while" (makes us look bad)
- "It's a known issue" (without explaining or offering help)
- "We're aware" (sounds dismissive)

### When We Need to Escalate

**Template:**
```
Thank you for your patience. This requires specialized assistance from our [team name] team.

I'm escalating your case to them now. A specialist will reach out within [timeframe] via email at [customer's email].

Your ticket reference is [TICKET-ID] if you need to follow up.

[If appropriate: In the meantime, here's what you can do: ...]
```

**Don't Say:**
- "I can't help you" (sounds like giving up)
- "You need to talk to someone else" (sounds dismissive)
- "That's above my pay grade" (unprofessional)

### When the Customer is Wrong (But Politely)

**Template:**
```
I can understand why that would seem like the case! Let me clarify how [feature] works.

[Explanation with evidence]

Does that make sense? Happy to explain further if needed!
```

**Don't Say:**
- "You're wrong"
- "That's not how it works" (too blunt)
- "If you had read the documentation..." (condescending)

### When Customer is Frustrated or Angry

**Template:**
```
I completely understand your frustration, and I sincerely apologize for [specific issue].

Here's what I'm going to do right now to help:
1. [Immediate action]
2. [Follow-up action]
3. [Prevention/future improvement]

You shouldn't have to deal with this. Let me make it right.
```

**Key Principles:**
- Acknowledge their feelings (don't dismiss)
- Apologize sincerely (even if it's not directly our fault)
- Take ownership
- Focus on solutions, not excuses
- Follow through on promises

**Don't Say:**
- "Calm down"
- "I understand" (without showing how)
- "Our policy is..." (sounds like you're hiding behind rules)
- "There's nothing I can do"
- "Other customers don't have this problem"

### When We Made a Mistake

**Template:**
```
You're absolutely right, and I apologize for the error in my previous message.

[Correct information]

Thank you for catching that! Let me know if you have any other questions.
```

**Don't Say:**
- "My bad" (too casual for most situations)
- Ignore the mistake and pretend it didn't happen
- Blame someone else ("The previous agent was wrong")

## Formatting Best Practices

### Use Numbered Lists for Steps

**Good:**
```
To export your data:
1. Go to Settings → Privacy
2. Click "Export Data"
3. You'll receive an email when ready (usually 24 hours)
4. Download the zip file from the link in the email
```

**Bad:**
```
To export your data, go to settings and then privacy and click export data. You'll get an email when it's ready, usually in about 24 hours. Then download the zip file from the link.
```

### Use Bold for Important Info

**Good:**
```
**IMPORTANT:** The reset link expires in 1 hour.
```

### Use Bullets for Lists (Non-Sequential)

**Good:**
```
Professional plan includes:
- Unlimited users
- API access
- Priority support
- Custom fields
```

### Break Up Long Text

**Good:**
```
I found a few things that might help:

**Password Issues**
Try the password reset link at [URL].

**Account Access**
Make sure your email is verified.

Let me know which applies to your situation!
```

**Bad:**
```
I found a few things that might help. If it's a password issue, try the password reset link. If it's account access, make sure your email is verified. Let me know which applies.
```

## Word Choice

### Preferred Words/Phrases

| ✅ Use This | ❌ Not This |
|------------|-------------|
| "I'll help you with that" | "I'll try to help" |
| "Here's how to..." | "You should..." |
| "I found..." | "I was able to find..." (wordy) |
| "Let me check on that" | "Let me see what I can do" |
| "You can..." | "You would need to..." |
| "Thanks for letting us know" | "Thank you for your feedback" (too formal) |
| "I apologize" | "Sorry about that" (too casual for serious issues) |
| "I understand this is frustrating" | "I understand your concern" (too clinical) |

### Words to Avoid

- **Jargon:** "utilize" (use "use"), "leverage" (use "use"), "at this time" (use "now")
- **Hedging:** "maybe", "perhaps", "possibly" (be confident)
- **Negative:** "can't", "won't", "unable to" (frame positively when possible)
- **Overly Formal:** "hereby", "aforementioned", "kindly", "please be advised"
- **Robotic:** "Your ticket has been received", "This issue is being processed"

## Channel-Specific Examples

### Email: Complex Technical Issue

```
Subject: Re: Integration with GitHub not syncing

Hi Taylor,

Thanks for reaching out about the GitHub integration! I can help you troubleshoot this.

I see you've already tried re-authorizing the connection, which is great - that's the first step I would have suggested.

Let's try a few more things:

**Step 1: Check Repository Access**
1. Go to Settings → Integrations → GitHub
2. Click "Manage Repositories"
3. Ensure the repository you want to sync is checked

**Step 2: Verify Branch Naming**
CloudFlow automatically links branches that include the task ID. Make sure your branch is named like:
- feature/TASK-123-description
- bugfix/TASK-456-fix

**Step 3: Check Permissions**
GitHub requires "repo" and "read:org" permissions. If you authorized with limited permissions, try:
1. Disconnect GitHub integration
2. Reconnect and authorize all permissions

If none of these work, the issue might be on GitHub's side. You can check their status page at githubstatus.com.

Let me know what happens after trying these steps, and I'll escalate to our integrations team if needed!

Best regards,
Alex Chen
CloudFlow Support Team

📚 Related docs:
- GitHub Integration Guide: [link]
- Troubleshooting Integrations: [link]
```

## Quality Checklist

Before sending any support message, check:

- [ ] **Did I address the customer by name?**
- [ ] **Did I acknowledge their specific issue/question?**
- [ ] **Did I provide clear, actionable steps?**
- [ ] **Did I use appropriate formatting (bullets, bold, numbers)?**
- [ ] **Did I match the tone to the channel (email/WhatsApp/web form)?**
- [ ] **Did I avoid jargon or explain technical terms?**
- [ ] **Did I proofread for typos and grammar?**
- [ ] **Did I offer further help or next steps?**
- [ ] **Did I sound helpful, not robotic?**
- [ ] **Would I want to receive this response if I were the customer?**

## AI Agent Specific Guidelines

### AI Agent Identity

**The AI agent should:**
- Identify as "CloudFlow Support Team" (not claim to be human)
- Be transparent: "I'm an AI assistant, but I can help you with [issue]"
- Know its limitations: "This requires specialized assistance, let me connect you with a team member"

**The AI agent should NOT:**
- Pretend to be human if directly asked ("Are you a bot?")
- Claim to have emotions or personal experiences
- Use phrases like "I'm having a bad day" or "I totally get it" (implies human experience)

### When to Mention AI Nature

**Proactively mention:**
- If asked directly: "Are you a bot?" → "Yes, I'm an AI assistant here to help! I can answer questions about CloudFlow and help troubleshoot issues."
- When escalating: "I'm connecting you with a human specialist who can..."

**Don't mention unless asked:**
- In routine support interactions (customer doesn't need to know, just needs help)

### Uncertainty Handling

When AI is not confident (confidence <0.7):

**Template:**
```
I found some information that might help, but I want to make sure you get the most accurate answer.

[Provide what you found with appropriate caveats]

For the most accurate guidance, I can connect you with a specialist. Would you like me to do that?
```
